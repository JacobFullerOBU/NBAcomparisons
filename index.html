⏱️ Iconic Moments

  • 1962 Wilt Chamberlain scores 100 points in a single game against the Knicks — an untouched record more than 60 years later.
  • 1986 Michael Jordan erupts for 63 points on the Celtics in the playoffs, still the all-time single-game playoff scoring record.
  • 1997 Jordan battles the flu to drop 38 points in Game 5 of the NBA Finals, lifting the Bulls to a series-clinching position.
  • 2016 The Cleveland Cavaliers become the first team to overcome a 3–1 Finals deficit, delivering Cleveland its first championship.
  • 2023 LeBron James surpasses Kareem Abdul-Jabbar's 39-year-old record to become the NBA's all-time leading scorer.
